MEET YOUR DAILY FIND

A better way to browse.

  1. The scanner does the hunting.

    Your Google Sheet collects matching sliBuy auctions. This page reads that public data without an API key or sign-in.

  2. You make it yours.

    Search, pick categories, and narrow the snapshot bids. Trash hides an item in this browser only; you can restore it anytime.

  3. Finish the hunt on sliBuy.

    Open an auction to check the live bid, condition, pickup details, fees, and official end time before bidding.

The next-refresh countdown targets 7 AM America/Chicago and adjusts for daylight saving time. The Google Apps Script scanner runs around that time and may finish later. This site checks the sheet every 5 minutes while open; it does not run the scanner.
