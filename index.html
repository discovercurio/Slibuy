MEET YOUR DAILY FIND

A better way to browse.

  1. The scanner does the hunting.

    Your Google Sheet collects matching sliBuy auctions. This page reads that public data without an API key or sign-in.

  2. You make it yours.

    Search, pick categories, and narrow the snapshot bids. Star a find to save it to Favorites; trash one to hide it. Both are kept in this browser only, and saved favorites stay listed even after they drop out of the next scan.

  3. Finish the hunt on sliBuy.

    Open an auction to check the live bid, condition, pickup details, fees, and official end time before bidding.

The next-scan countdown targets 7 AM America/Chicago and adjusts for daylight saving time. The Google Apps Script scanner runs around that time and may finish later. This site checks the sheet every 5 minutes while open; it does not run the scanner.
